Understanding Mainland

What Is Sharjah Mainland Company Formation?

Sharjah Mainland Company Formation allows businesses to establish an operating company licensed by the Sharjah Economic Development Department (SEDD).

Sharjah Mainland businesses are licensed through SEDD for approved economic activities. This structure can be particularly relevant for businesses requiring direct UAE market access, commercial premises, local operations or flexibility beyond a specific free-zone jurisdiction.

  • Trading and commercial
  • Professional and consulting
  • Industrial activities
  • Retail
  • Contracting and construction
  • Technology and business services
  • Tourism-related activities
  • Other approved activities
Built around your operating model. The exact licence, legal form and approvals depend on the proposed activity and ownership structure.

Structure Before Registration

Legal Structures for Sharjah Mainland Businesses

Sharjah's mainland framework provides different legal forms depending on the activity, ownership and business model.

Limited Liability Company — LLC

A commonly used structure for eligible mainland activities. An LLC may have two or more shareholders, up to 50, subject to applicable legal requirements.

One-Person Company

A single natural or legal person can establish an eligible one-person limited-liability company.

Sole Establishment

An individual-owned establishment may be available for eligible activities and ownership structures.

Professional Business Company

A professional structure may be available for eligible activities, subject to ownership and service-agent rules.

Branch

Existing UAE, GCC or foreign companies may establish eligible branches, subject to applicable requirements.

Assess the legal form before incorporation

Select the appropriate legal form based on the activity, ownership and intended business model.

Preparation & Registration

Sharjah Mainland Company Formation Requirements

Depending on the activity and legal structure, requirements may include:

  • Passport copies of shareholders
  • Emirates ID or UAE visa documents, where applicable
  • Proposed economic activity
  • Trade name
  • Initial approval
  • Technical evaluation
  • Memorandum of Association, where applicable
  • Lease or tenancy documentation, where required
  • Corporate shareholder documents
  • Ultimate Beneficial Owner information
  • External approvals for regulated activities
SEDD requirements are activity-specific

Current issuance requirements may include the trade-name certificate, technical evaluation report, certified lease documents, activity-specific approvals and applicable legal contracts. Requirements vary by activity, legal form, ownership and premises.

Before You Proceed

Important Considerations Before Setting Up

Confirm the regulatory position for your activity, ownership and premises before committing to a structure.

Foreign Ownership

100% foreign ownership is available for most eligible commercial and industrial activities, subject to exclusions.

Restricted Activities

Strategic-impact and regulated sectors can have different ownership and approval requirements, including certain security, defence, banking, insurance and telecom activities.

Professional Activities

Certain professional structures may have separate ownership or UAE service-agent requirements.

Technical Evaluation

SEDD may require a technical evaluation of the proposed location, depending on the activity and premises.

Office Requirements

Premises requirements vary. Eligible office-based activities may use SEDD's Instant Licence without a location or lease contract during the first year.

Corporate Tax

Sharjah Mainland companies are subject to the UAE Corporate Tax framework.

VAT

VAT registration and compliance should be assessed under the applicable UAE VAT rules.

Additional Approvals

Food, industrial, healthcare, education, tourism and other regulated activities may need authority approvals.
