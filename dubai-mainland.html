Understanding Mainland

What Is Dubai Mainland?

A Dubai Mainland company is an onshore business licensed through Dubai's Department of Economy and Tourism (DET) for approved economic activities.

Unlike a business established within a specific free zone, a Mainland company can generally operate across Dubai and the wider UAE, subject to applicable regulations and activity-specific requirements.

Relevant for local commercial operations, customer access, physical premises, trading, distribution and services—with flexibility for growth.

Preparation & Registration

Formation Requirements & Documentation

Exact requirements vary with the activity, legal structure, shareholders, premises and any additional regulatory approvals.

✓Passport and identification documents
✓Visa and Emirates ID, where applicable
✓Proposed activities and trade name
✓Initial approval
✓Memorandum of Association, where applicable
✓Corporate shareholder documents, where relevant
✓Tenancy or premises documents, where required

Initial approval alone does not permit business operations. Operations may begin only after obtaining the required licence and regulatory approvals. Processing times depend on documentation, business activity, company structure, premises and authority approvals.

Plan Your Investment

What affects setup costs?

Costs are based on the specific company and scope, rather than a universal fixed price.

  • Business activity and licence type
  • Legal structure and number of shareholders
  • Premises and tenancy requirements
  • Visa and immigration requirements
  • External approvals
  • Government charges and third-party expenses

Post-formation obligations

Requirements after incorporation may include:

  • UAE Corporate Tax registration and applicable filings
  • VAT registration and compliance, where applicable
  • Visa and immigration requirements
  • Bank account application and bank KYC
  • Licence renewals and company amendments
  • Other applicable corporate and regulatory obligations
