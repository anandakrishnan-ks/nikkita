Overview

What Is SHAMS?

Sharjah Media City is a Sharjah free zone established in 2017 to support media, creative and entrepreneurial businesses.

Today, SHAMS supports startups, SMEs, companies and freelancers across media, digital, professional and other approved business areas. It can be particularly relevant for businesses seeking a flexible and digitally oriented environment in Sharjah.

Media & Content Digital Marketing Technology & IT E-commerce Professional Services Creative Industries Education & Training
Built for emerging ideas. Activity, structure, workspace and visa requirements should be assessed together.
Assess the Fit

Is SHAMS Right for Your Business?

SHAMS is strongest when its flexible, digitally oriented environment matches the actual operating model.

Who May Consider SHAMS

  • Media and content businesses
  • Digital marketing and communications companies
  • Technology, IT and e-commerce businesses
  • Creative professionals and freelancers
  • Consulting and professional services
  • Startups, SMEs and entrepreneurs

When SHAMS May Not Be the Right Fit

  • Substantial warehousing or industrial infrastructure is required
  • The primary requirement is large-scale manufacturing
  • A Dubai-based jurisdiction or ecosystem is essential
  • A specialised trading or industrial zone offers a stronger fit
Select the jurisdiction around the operating requirement

The right choice depends on the business activity, operating model and real infrastructure needs—not the licence price alone.

Important Considerations

Before Setting Up in SHAMS

Free-zone status does not remove UAE tax, regulatory or activity-specific obligations.

Corporate Tax

SHAMS companies fall within the UAE Corporate Tax framework.

Qualifying Free Zone Person

0% is not a blanket exemption; it applies only to qualifying income when all conditions are met.

VAT

Registration and compliance should be assessed under applicable UAE VAT rules.

Mainland Business

A SHAMS licence should not be treated as an unrestricted mainland operating licence.

Additional Approvals

Certain activities may require approvals or NOCs from relevant authorities.

Workspace & Visas

Requirements depend on the selected setup and operating needs.

Tax treatment depends on current legislation and the company's specific facts. Independent tax advice should be obtained for material decisions.
