JAFZA Offshore Company Formation

Establish Your JAFZA Offshore Company with Clarity and Confidence

An international corporate structure for holding, investment, international trade and cross-border business arrangements.

Nikki Advisory helps clients assess whether JAFZA Offshore is appropriate for their ownership, business purpose, assets and international requirements.

Start Your JAFZA Offshore Setup

JAFZA Offshore is not JAFZA Free Zone. It is not a conventional UAE operating licence.

Overview

What Is JAFZA Offshore?

JAFZA Offshore provides a UAE-registered corporate structure for eligible international business, holding, investment and cross-border arrangements.

The proposed purpose should be assessed against the applicable JAFZA Offshore Regulations and laws of the jurisdictions involved.

Offshore ≠ Free Zone Operating Company

For ordinary UAE commercial operations, compare an appropriate JAFZA Free Zone or mainland company.

Business Fit

Is JAFZA Offshore Right for Your Business?

Start with where the activity takes place and what the entity needs to hold.

Suitable For

  • International entrepreneurs
  • Investors needing a holding structure
  • International trading businesses
  • Groups needing a UAE-registered holding entity
  • Eligible asset ownership
  • Cross-border corporate arrangements

May Not Be the Right Fit

  • Ordinary commercial activities within the UAE
  • A UAE mainland operating licence is needed
  • A conventional JAFZA Free Zone company is required
  • UAE-based employees or operational facilities are essential
  • A physical warehouse, factory or operating office is needed
Purpose before price

Select an offshore structure for the company's actual purpose—not merely as a lower-cost alternative to an operating company.

Important Considerations

Before Establishing a JAFZA Offshore Company

UAE Operations

JAFZA does not issue an operating business licence to an offshore company; ordinary commercial activity with persons in the UAE is restricted.

Registered Agent

New offshore registration is processed through a JAFZA Registered Agent.

Banking

Incorporation does not guarantee a corporate account; banks conduct separate due diligence.

Corporate Tax

Offshore status does not automatically confer UAE Corporate Tax exemption.

International Tax

Other countries' rules may apply to shareholders, assets, income or related businesses.

UBO & AML

Applicable beneficial-owner and anti-money-laundering obligations must be maintained.

See JAFZA's offshore guide, UBO guidance and the Federal Tax Authority's FAQs.
