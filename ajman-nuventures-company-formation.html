Overview

What Is Ajman NuVentures Centre Free Zone?

Ajman NuVentures Centre Free Zone (ANCFZ) is a digital-focused free zone in Ajman designed to provide simplified business setup solutions for entrepreneurs, startups, SMEs and growing businesses.

The free zone focuses on technology, innovation, digital business, professional services, trading, media and emerging sectors, supported by a streamlined online setup process.

Is Ajman NuVentures Right for Your Business?

ANCFZ may be suitable for businesses looking for a simplified digital setup, multiple activities and flexible business solutions.

Important Considerations

Before Setting Up in ANCFZ

Corporate Tax

ANCFZ companies fall within the UAE Corporate Tax regime. Treatment depends on activities, income and eligibility under the Free Zone rules.

0% Corporate Tax

The 0% rate is not automatic. It applies to Qualifying Income of a Qualifying Free Zone Person, subject to the applicable conditions.

VAT

VAT registration and compliance should be assessed according to the applicable UAE VAT rules.

Mainland Business

A free-zone licence does not automatically provide unrestricted mainland operating rights. The appropriate arrangement should be assessed according to the intended activity.

Additional Approvals

Certain regulated activities may require approvals or NOCs from relevant authorities.

Workspace & Visas

Requirements depend on the selected package and business requirements.

For current tax rules, see the Federal Tax Authority's Free Zone Persons guidance.
