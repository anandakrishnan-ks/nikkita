Dubai's international business landscape
Overview

What Is DIFC?

Dubai International Financial Centre (DIFC) is a leading financial centre in Dubai serving the Middle East, Africa and South Asia (MEASA) region.

DIFC provides an internationally recognised legal and regulatory environment, dedicated business infrastructure and corporate structures for financial, professional, investment and wealth-related activities.

A specialised financial centreAn internationally oriented business environment in the heart of Dubai.

Key Business Areas

Financial Services
Wealth & Asset Management
Banking & Investment
FinTech & Innovation
Professional Services
Family Offices & Private Wealth
Holding & Investment Structures
Technology & Specialised Businesses

Financial services activities are subject to the applicable DFSA regulatory requirements.

Assess the Fit

Who May Consider DIFC?

DIFC may be relevant for businesses that require a specialised financial-centre environment, internationally recognised legal framework or sophisticated corporate structure.

Who May Consider DIFC

  • Financial services businesses
  • Asset and wealth management firms
  • FinTech and innovation businesses
  • Family offices and private wealth structures
  • Investment and holding structures
  • Professional and corporate service businesses
  • Technology businesses seeking a specialised Dubai ecosystem
  • Businesses targeting regional and international markets

When DIFC May Not Be the Right Fit

  • Your primary objective is the lowest-cost company formation.
  • Your business does not require a specialised financial-centre environment.
  • Your activity is better suited to a general commercial free zone.
  • Your business requires substantial industrial, warehouse or manufacturing infrastructure.

The right jurisdiction should be selected according to the business activity, structure and long-term objectives.

Licensing & Approval

DIFC Licence Options

DIFC licensing requirements vary depending on whether the proposed activity is regulated or non-regulated.

Financial Services Licences

DFSA authorisation required

For regulated activities such as banking, asset management, investment, brokerage, custody and other financial services. Activities are subject to DFSA authorisation and regulatory requirements.

Professional / Non-Financial Licences

DIFC registration requirements

For approved professional and commercial activities that do not constitute regulated financial services.

Innovation & Start-up Licences

Eligibility applies

For eligible innovation and technology businesses.

Representative Office

Permitted activities only

For eligible businesses seeking a DIFC presence for permitted representative activities.

Specialised Structures

DIFC provides specialised structures including Family Offices, Foundations, Holding Companies, Managing Offices, Proprietary Investment structures and Prescribed Companies, subject to applicable eligibility requirements. Regulated and non-regulated DIFC activities follow different approval requirements.

Corporate Structures

DIFC Company Structure Options

Available options serve different operational, ownership and wealth-structuring objectives.

DIFC Private Company

A DIFC company can be established as an independent legal entity for eligible business activities.

Branch

An existing UAE or foreign company may establish a branch in DIFC, subject to the applicable requirements.

Representative Office

An eligible foreign company may establish a representative office for permitted representative activities.

Specialised Corporate Structures

Depending on the purpose, DIFC provides structures such as Foundation, Holding Company, Family Office, Managing Office, Prescribed Company and Proprietary Investment Structure.

The appropriate structure depends on the purpose, ownership, activity and regulatory requirements.

Preparation

DIFC Company Formation Requirements

Depending on the proposed structure and activity, requirements may include:

Requirements vary significantly between regulated, non-regulated and specialised DIFC structures.

  • Passport copies of shareholders and directors
  • Proof of address
  • Proposed business activity
  • Proposed company name
  • Business plan or business profile, where applicable
  • Corporate documents for corporate shareholders
  • Ultimate beneficial owner (UBO) information
  • Regulatory documentation for regulated activities
  • Additional documents or approvals where required

Important Considerations

Before Setting Up in DIFC

Financial Services Regulation

Financial services activities require the appropriate DFSA authorisation and must comply with the applicable regulatory framework.

Corporate Tax

DIFC entities fall within the UAE Corporate Tax regime. A Qualifying Free Zone Person may be subject to 0% Corporate Tax on Qualifying Income, subject to applicable conditions.

0% Corporate Tax Is Not Automatic

The applicable tax treatment depends on the entity, activities, income and satisfaction of the relevant conditions.

Capital Requirements

Capital requirements vary according to the licence and sector, particularly for regulated financial activities.

Additional Approvals

Certain activities require regulatory or other authority approvals before the business can operate.

Business Facility

Office requirements depend on the selected licence, structure and business requirements.
