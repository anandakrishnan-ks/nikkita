Dubai South Free Zone Company Formation

A strategically located business hub (Dubai World Central – DWC) designed for logistics, trade, e-commerce, and scalable global operations.

Book a Consultation Talk to an Advisor

Overview

Dubai South Free Zone (DWC) is a master-planned economic ecosystem centered around Al Maktoum International Airport and the Logistics Corridor, connecting sea, land, and air transport.

Core Strengths:

Unmatched Logistics Connectivity

  • Direct connection to Al Maktoum Airport & Jebel Ali Port
  • Dedicated logistics & bonded customs corridor
  • Direct access to E311 & E66 major transport arteries

Integrated Facilities

  • Smart flexi-desks, modern offices & industrial plots
  • EZDubai dedicated e-commerce zone for warehousing
  • Scalable visa quotas based on lease terms

Cost Efficiency

  • Competitive entry licensing cost in Dubai
  • 100% foreign ownership & 0% personal tax
  • Streamlined digital government registration

Dubai South provides operations-driven enterprises with unmatched supply chain advantages.
