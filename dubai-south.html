Overview

What Is Dubai South?

Dubai South is an integrated business and economic development in Dubai built around aviation, logistics, trade and business infrastructure.

Its Free Zone supports businesses across multiple sectors, while the wider ecosystem includes dedicated Logistics, Aviation, Business Park and e-commerce infrastructure. More than 4,200 companies operate within Dubai South.

Is Dubai South Right for Your Business?

Dubai South can be particularly relevant for businesses that value strategic connectivity, logistics infrastructure and room for operational growth.

When Another Jurisdiction May Be Better

  • The primary objective is the lowest-cost free-zone setup
  • The business does not benefit from its logistics or strategic location
  • A highly specialised financial or sector-specific jurisdiction is required
  • The business requires infrastructure better suited to another industrial or specialised free zone

Important Considerations

Before Setting Up in Dubai South

Corporate Tax

Dubai South Free Zone companies fall within the UAE Corporate Tax regime. Applicable treatment depends on activities, income and eligibility under the Free Zone rules.

0% Corporate Tax

The 0% rate is not automatic. It applies to Qualifying Income of a Qualifying Free Zone Person, subject to the applicable conditions.

VAT

VAT registration and compliance should be assessed according to the applicable UAE VAT rules.

Mainland Business

A free-zone licence does not automatically provide unrestricted mainland operating rights. The appropriate arrangement should be assessed according to the intended activity.

Additional Approvals

Aviation, logistics, industrial and other regulated activities may require additional approvals.

Facility Requirements

Office, warehouse or specialised facility requirements depend on the selected activity and operating model.

For current tax rules, see the Federal Tax Authority's Free Zone Persons guidance.
