Before you apply

UAE business setup questions

How do I choose between mainland, Free Zone and offshore setup?

Compare your intended activity, customers, ownership, visa needs and operating requirements before choosing a route. This guide outlines mainland and Free Zone operating options and offshore structuring; business structuring guidance can help clarify the decision.

What documents should I prepare for UAE company formation?

The existing setup guides list starting documents such as a passport copy and business or application details. The exact checklist depends on the authority, activity and structure. Review the mainland documents, Free Zone documents or offshore documents for the route you are considering.

What does the business setup process involve?

The route-specific guides describe activity and jurisdiction selection, name reservation or approval, documentation and application, followed by licensing or incorporation. Office arrangements, visas and additional approvals depend on the route. See the cost and process guides for the relevant sequence.

How much does UAE business setup cost?

Costs vary by jurisdiction, business activity, company structure, visa needs, premises and approvals. Published figures are indicative planning information, not a final quotation. Compare mainland, Free Zone and offshore setup costs, then request a quotation for your requirements.

How long does formation take, and who is eligible?

There is no single timeline or eligibility rule for every setup. The authority, activity, ownership structure, complete documentation and required approvals determine what applies. The route-specific guides provide indicative timelines; discuss your circumstances before relying on a schedule.

What support is available after company formation?

Nikki Advisory’s business services include PRO and government coordination, corporate compliance, accounting and bookkeeping, business support and company closure coordination, depending on the agreed scope.
