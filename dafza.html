Overview

What Is DAFZA?

Dubai Airport Freezone was established in 1996 and is strategically located next to Dubai International Airport.

DAFZA provides a structured free-zone environment for businesses operating across regional and international markets, particularly where connectivity, trade, logistics and expansion are important.

Assess the Fit

Who May Consider DAFZA?

DAFZA is most relevant when its Dubai location, international connectivity and facility options add practical value.

Import, Export & Re-export

Professional & Consulting Firms

Technology & E-commerce

Warehouse & Industrial Users

Regional & International Operations

Consider the Alternative

When DAFZA May Not Be the Right Fit

  • Your priority is the lowest-cost free-zone setup.
  • Your activity gains little from DAFZA's location or infrastructure.
  • Your business does not require a Dubai free-zone presence.
  • You need mainland operations without planning the applicable regulatory route.

The right jurisdiction depends on your activity, operating model and long-term objectives—not simply the licence cost.

Choose the Right Permission

Licence Options in DAFZA

The licence should match the proposed activity and intended operating model.

Trade Licence

For approved import, export, re-export, distribution and storage of specified products.

Service Licence

For approved service-oriented and professional activities.

Industrial Licence

For approved light manufacturing, packaging and assembling.

E-Commerce Licence

For approved online trading of goods and services.

General Trading Licence

For eligible general trading across approved product categories.

Specialised Routes

Options outside the standard licence grid

These routes have distinct eligibility, purpose and operating requirements.

Dual Licence with DET

Eligible DAFZ companies may apply for a DET licence under the applicable framework.

Talent Pass

An individual-focused route for eligible professionals and freelancers in approved fields.

Family Office Licences

Single-Family Office and Multi-Family Office routes for eligible activities.

Assess the activity before choosing a licence route

Not every route is relevant to every business, and specialised options should not be treated as ordinary company-formation licences.

Select the Legal Form

DAFZA Company Structure Options

Ownership, existing corporate presence and long-term objectives determine the appropriate structure.

FZCO — Free Zone Company

A DAFZ Freezone Company may be formed with 1–50 individual or non-individual shareholders.

Minimum share capital: AED 1

PLC — Public Limited Company

A limited-liability structure with no stated shareholder limit, intended for eligible businesses seeking to offer or list shares.

Subject to applicable requirements

Branch

A foreign company may establish a DAFZA branch of its existing company.

No share capital required

AED 1 is the stated minimum share capital for an FZCO; it is not the total cost of setting up a DAFZA company.

Tax & Regulatory Considerations

What Should Businesses Consider?

Free-zone status does not remove UAE tax, compliance or activity-specific obligations.

Corporate Tax

DAFZA companies remain within the UAE Corporate Tax framework.

Qualifying Free Zone Person

0% Corporate Tax may apply to qualifying income when all applicable conditions are met.

Tax Compliance

Free-zone status is not an automatic exemption from registration, filing and compliance.

Mainland Business

Activity outside the free zone remains subject to applicable UAE commercial requirements.

Additional Approvals

Certain activities may require approvals or NOCs from relevant authorities.

Business Space

Facility requirements depend on activity, structure and operational needs.

Tax treatment depends on current legislation and the company's specific facts. Independent tax advice should be obtained for material decisions.
