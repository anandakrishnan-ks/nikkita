KIZAD Company Formation in Abu Dhabi

A premier industrial and logistics economic zone (KEZAD) integrated with Khalifa Port for manufacturing, trade, and distribution.

Book a Consultation Talk to an Advisor

Overview

KIZAD (Khalifa Industrial Zone Abu Dhabi / KEZAD) is Abu Dhabi's flagship industrial hub directly integrated with Khalifa Port. It offers world-class port infrastructure, low utility costs, and heavy industrial facilities for global trade.

KIZAD Key Highlights:

Khalifa Port Integration

  • Direct sea transport connectivity to deep-water Khalifa Port
  • Strategic location midway between Abu Dhabi and Dubai
  • Seamless highway freight access across GCC markets

Industrial & Modular Facilities

  • Pre-built modular warehouses & cold storage units
  • Serviced industrial land plots with long-term leases
  • Competitive utility rates (power, water, gas)

Flexible Licensing

  • Free Zone & Dual-Licensing options with ADDED
  • 100% foreign ownership & 0% personal tax
  • Fast-track setup for regional distribution & assembly

KIZAD is the ideal hub for heavy manufacturing, food processing, and maritime logistics.
