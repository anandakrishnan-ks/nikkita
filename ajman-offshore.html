Ajman Offshore Company Formation

An economical, fast-track offshore option in the UAE designed for basic international holding structures and cross-border invoicing.

Book a Consultation Talk to an Advisor

Overview

Ajman Offshore is regulated under the Ajman Free Zone Authority. It offers one of the fastest and most budget-friendly offshore setups in the UAE, suitable for simple holding structures and non-resident international activities.

Ajman Offshore Highlights:

Budget-Friendly Offshore

  • One of the lowest entry and renewal fee structures in the UAE
  • Fast turn-around incorporation (typically 3 working days)
  • 100% foreign ownership & capital repatriation

Asset & Equity Protection

  • Hold shares in foreign companies & offshore bank accounts
  • Confidential corporate shareholder registry
  • 0% corporate & personal income tax

Key Limitations

  • Cannot trade locally within the onshore UAE market
  • Does NOT issue UAE residency visas or local office leases
  • Requires setup via an official Registered Agent

Ajman Offshore offers low maintenance costs for international trade & asset isolation.
