Ajman Offshore Company Formation

Establish Your Ajman Offshore Company with Clarity and Confidence

An offshore corporate structure for eligible international business, holding and cross-border activities conducted outside the UAE.

Nikki Advisory helps clients assess whether an Ajman Offshore structure is appropriate for their business purpose, ownership and international requirements.

Start Your Ajman Offshore Setup

Ajman Offshore is not an Ajman Free Zone operating company. It should not be used as a substitute for a UAE operating licence.

Overview

What Is Ajman Offshore?

Ajman Offshore is a UAE-registered offshore corporate structure intended for eligible international activities, holding and cross-border arrangements conducted outside the UAE.

Permitted use depends on the proposed purpose, ownership structure and applicable regulations. The business model should be reviewed before incorporation.

Ajman Offshore ≠ UAE Operating Licence

If the plan requires ordinary UAE commercial operations, assess an appropriate Ajman Free Zone or mainland route instead.

Business Fit

Is Ajman Offshore Right for Your Business?

Start with the business purpose and where the activity will actually take place.

Suitable For

  • International entrepreneurs
  • Investors considering an eligible holding structure
  • Activities conducted outside the UAE
  • International trading structures
  • Cross-border corporate arrangements

May Not Be the Right Fit

  • Ordinary business operations are planned within the UAE
  • A UAE mainland operating licence is needed
  • A conventional UAE Free Zone company is required
  • UAE-based employees or operational facilities are essential
  • The activity requires a specific regulated UAE licence
Assess the purpose first

An offshore structure should follow the actual activities and ownership needs—not lead them.

Important Considerations

Before Incorporating an Ajman Offshore Company

UAE Operations

Offshore incorporation does not automatically permit ordinary commercial operations in the UAE.

Banking

Bank-account approval is not guaranteed; each bank conducts its own KYC and due diligence.

Corporate Tax

Offshore status does not itself confer a UAE Corporate Tax exemption.

International Tax

Other countries' rules may apply where shareholders, assets, income or activities are located.

UBO Compliance

Applicable beneficial-owner records and obligations must be maintained.

Regulated Activities

Some activities need approvals or may not suit an offshore structure.

See the Federal Tax Authority's Corporate Tax FAQs and Ajman's UBO guidance. Specific tax and legal treatment requires individual assessment.
