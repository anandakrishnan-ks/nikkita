Understanding Mainland

What Is Abu Dhabi Mainland Company Formation?

Abu Dhabi Mainland allows businesses to establish an operating company licensed by the Abu Dhabi Department of Economic Development (ADDED).

A mainland structure can be particularly relevant for businesses requiring direct UAE market access, local operations, commercial premises, employees or flexibility beyond a specific free-zone jurisdiction.

Licence Types in Abu Dhabi Mainland

ADDED provides different economic licence categories based on the proposed activity.

Commercial Licence

For approved trading, retail, distribution and other commercial activities.

Professional Licence

For approved professional and service-based activities.

Industrial Licence

For approved manufacturing and industrial activities.

Tourism Licence

For eligible tourism-related activities.

Agricultural Licence

For eligible agricultural activities.

Occupational Licence

For applicable occupational activities.

Freelancer and Virtual Licences are specialised routes and should not be confused with standard mainland company formation. The appropriate route depends on the proposed activity and requirements.

Structure Before Registration

Legal Structures for Abu Dhabi Mainland Businesses

The available legal form depends on the activity, ownership and intended business model.

Limited Liability Company

A commonly used mainland structure, subject to activity and legal requirements.

Sole Establishment

An individual-owned establishment may suit eligible activities.

Partnership

Partnership structures may be available for eligible activities and arrangements.

Branch

An existing UAE or foreign company may establish an eligible branch.

Other Legal Forms

Additional forms may be available according to activity and ownership.

Assess the legal form before incorporation. The structure affects ownership, liability, documentation and future operations.

Preparation & Registration

Abu Dhabi Mainland Company Formation Requirements

Depending on the activity and legal structure, requirements may include:

  • Passport copies of shareholders
  • Emirates ID or UAE visa documents, where applicable
  • Proposed economic activity
  • Trade name
  • Initial approval
  • Memorandum of Association, where applicable
  • Lease or tenancy documentation, where required
  • Corporate shareholder documents
  • Ultimate Beneficial Owner information
  • External approvals for regulated activities

Requirements are activity-specific. Documentation varies according to the activity, legal form, ownership and premises requirements.
