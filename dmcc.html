Understanding DMCC

What Is DMCC Company Formation?

Company formation in Dubai Multi Commodities Centre (DMCC) allows entrepreneurs and companies to establish a licensed free-zone entity in Dubai's Jumeirah Lakes Towers business district.

The jurisdiction supports a broad range of commercial, professional and industrial activities. Its digital setup process, business facilities and international ecosystem can make it relevant for companies building regional or global operations from Dubai.

Choose the Right Permission

DMCC Licence Types

The licence must match the activities the company intends to conduct. Regulated activities may need additional approval.

Trading Licence

For approved import, export, distribution and trading activities.

Service Licence

For approved consulting, professional and business services.

Commercial Licence

Used for eligible commercial structures and approved business purposes.

Industrial Licence

For approved production, processing and industrial activities.

Freelance Licence

For eligible individuals delivering approved professional services.

Specialised Structures

DMCC also supports eligible Special Purpose Vehicles, holding companies and family-office structures. Their purpose, activity permissions and facility requirements differ from standard operating companies.

Confirm activities before reserving the structure

Activity wording affects licensing, external approvals, office requirements, banking and tax analysis.

Structure Before Registration

Company Structures Available in DMCC

Select the legal form according to ownership, existing group arrangements and the intended operating model.

DMCC Limited Liability Company

A separate legal entity formed by one or more individual or corporate shareholders, subject to DMCC requirements.

Branch

An extension of an existing parent company rather than a separate legal entity.

Subsidiary

A DMCC entity owned by an existing corporate shareholder, suitable for eligible group expansion plans.

The best structure depends on more than ownership

Liability, governance, document legalization, tax position and banking expectations should be assessed before filing.

Prepare the Application

DMCC Company Formation Requirements

Exact requirements vary by shareholder type, legal form, activity and regulatory profile. A typical application may include:

  • Passport copies of shareholders and managers
  • UAE visa or Emirates ID, where applicable
  • Proposed company names and business activities
  • Residential-address and contact information
  • Corporate shareholder documents, where applicable
  • Ultimate Beneficial Owner information
  • Business plan or third-party approvals, where requested
Corporate documents may need attestation or legalization

Requirements depend on where documents were issued and on the proposed shareholder and branch structure.

DMCC Regulatory & Tax Considerations

Free-zone status does not remove the need to assess UAE tax, financial-reporting and activity-specific obligations.

Corporate Tax Registration

DMCC companies fall within the UAE Corporate Tax framework and should assess registration and filing obligations.

Qualifying Free Zone Person

A 0% Corporate Tax rate can apply to qualifying income only when all statutory conditions are met; other taxable income may be taxed at 9%.

VAT & Tax Compliance

VAT registration and ongoing compliance should be assessed against the company's activities and thresholds.

Audited Financial Statements

Annual financial statements and audit obligations should be planned for; audited statements are also a condition for qualifying free-zone treatment.

External Approvals

Regulated goods and services may require approvals from additional UAE authorities.

Mainland Business

How a DMCC company conducts business outside the free zone depends on the activity, contracting model and applicable UAE rules.

Tax treatment depends on current legislation, Cabinet and Ministerial decisions, and the company's facts. Independent tax advice should be obtained for material decisions.
