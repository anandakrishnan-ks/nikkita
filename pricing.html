Tax considerations

Corporate Tax is generally 9% on taxable income above AED 375,000. Qualifying Free Zone Persons may have different treatment for qualifying income. VAT is 5%; mandatory registration generally applies when taxable supplies and imports exceed AED 375,000.

General guidance only; confirm current rules and eligibility with the authority or a qualified adviser. UAE Federal Tax Authority
02 / Saudi Arabia

A structured route to a long-term presence.

Saudi Arabia is suitable for serious expansion and long-term operational presence. It is a compliance-driven market, with office setup and staged approvals to plan for.

Indicative costSAR 25,000 OnwardsVaries by approvals, activity and structure. Approvals or documentation may extend the timeline.
Timeline4 – 8 weeksMay extend with approvals or documentation.

Terms & Conditions apply.

Process

  1. 01Business structure definition
  2. 02Investment licence approval via the Ministry of Investment
  3. 03Trade name & documentation
  4. 04Commercial Registration (CR)
  5. 05Government registrations
  6. 06Office setup & bank account
  7. 07Operational activation

Documents

  • Passport copy
  • Business plan
  • Company profile / parent documents
Important

Office setup required. Not a quick or low-cost entry.

Tax considerations

Corporate income tax is generally 20% on the non-Saudi ownership share of taxable income. Zakat is generally 2.5% of the applicable base for Saudi/GCC ownership. Standard VAT is 15%; registration depends on applicable thresholds.

General guidance only; confirm current rules and eligibility with the authority or a qualified adviser. ZATCA
