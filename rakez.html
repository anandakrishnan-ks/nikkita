RAKEZ Company Formation in UAE

A highly cost-efficient and customizable economic zone in Ras Al Khaimah offering commercial, industrial, and service licensing solutions.

Book a Consultation Talk to an Advisor

Overview

RAKEZ (Ras Al Khaimah Economic Zone) is one of the UAE’s largest economic zones, hosting over 18,000+ companies. It is widely recognized for its cost efficiency, versatile facility options (from flexi-desks to large industrial warehouses), and fast-track setup.

RAKEZ Key Highlights:

High Cost Efficiency

  • One of the most competitive entry cost structures in the UAE
  • Low cost multi-year license packages
  • 100% foreign ownership & 0% personal tax

Versatile Facilities

  • Flexi-desks, co-working, dedicated offices & industrial land
  • Customizable warehouses for light assembly & manufacturing
  • Dual-license options for mainland expansion

Fast Processing

  • Incorporation within 3 to 7 working days
  • Multiple activities allowed under one license
  • Hassle-free visa allocation & management

RAKEZ provides scalable economic solutions for startups, traders, and manufacturing firms alike.

  • Best For: Consultants & Freelancers

Industrial License

Manufacturing & Assembly
  • Hub: Industrial Zones
  • Ownership: 100% Foreign
  • Office: Warehouse / Land Plot
  • Best For: Light Factories & Logistics
