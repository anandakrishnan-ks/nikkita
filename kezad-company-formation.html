Overview

What Is KEZAD?

KEZAD Group provides an integrated business environment combining Free Zone and Domestic Economic Zone facilities with industrial, logistics and commercial infrastructure.

The Free Zone is particularly relevant for manufacturing, trading, import/export, logistics, warehousing and re-export, as well as selected professional and business services.

Licence & Activity Options

Licence Options in KEZAD

KEZAD provides three principal company licence categories. The appropriate licence depends on the proposed activity and operating model.

Industrial Licence

For approved import of raw materials, manufacturing, processing, assembly, packaging and export of intermediate or finished products.

Trading / General Trading

For approved import, export, distribution, stocking and warehousing. General Trading applies where product or product-group criteria are met.

Service Licence

For approved consulting, marketing, logistics support, freight forwarding, catering, travel and other permitted services.

Confirm the activity first

Permitted activities and any specialised approvals should be checked before applying. Review KEZAD's official licence categories.

Before Setting Up in KEZAD

Choose the right jurisdiction

Free Zone or Domestic Economic Zone?

KEZAD offers both. Free Zone structures can suit re-export, warehousing and internationally focused projects; the Domestic Economic Zone offers direct access to UAE and GCC markets. The route should follow where and how the business will operate.

Compare KEZAD jurisdictions

Facility Requirements

Manufacturing, logistics and industrial activities may require suitable facilities, utilities and operational approvals.

Free Zone Ownership

KEZAD's Free Zone permits 100% foreign ownership for eligible companies.

UAE Local Market

Free Zone businesses may access the local market through applicable distributors, local agents or available dual-licence arrangements, subject to requirements.

Corporate Tax

KEZAD Free Zone companies fall within the UAE Corporate Tax regime. Treatment depends on activities, income and Free Zone eligibility.

0% Corporate Tax

Free Zone status does not automatically provide a 0% rate. It applies to Qualifying Income of a Qualifying Free Zone Person, subject to conditions.

VAT & Customs

VAT obligations and customs treatment depend on the transactions and whether goods remain in the Free Zone or enter other markets.

See KEZAD's Free Zone guidance and the Federal Tax Authority's Free Zone Persons guidance.
