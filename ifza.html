Overview

What Is IFZA?

International Free Zone Authority (IFZA) is a Dubai free zone offering flexible company formation solutions for entrepreneurs, startups, SMEs and international businesses.

Its broad activity range, licence options and remote incorporation process make IFZA relevant for businesses seeking a Dubai presence without necessarily needing a physical office from the outset.

Important Considerations

Before Setting Up in IFZA

Workspace

IFZA says basic packages with 0–3 visa allocations can start without physical office space. Higher allocations or specific activities may change workspace needs.

Corporate Tax

IFZA companies fall within the UAE Corporate Tax regime. Treatment depends on activities, income and Free Zone eligibility.

0% Corporate Tax

The 0% rate is not automatic. It applies to Qualifying Income of a Qualifying Free Zone Person, subject to conditions.

VAT

VAT registration and compliance should be assessed under applicable UAE VAT rules.

Mainland Business

An IFZA Free Zone licence does not automatically provide unrestricted mainland operating rights.

Additional Approvals

Certain activities may require third-party or regulatory approvals.

See IFZA's workspace guidance and the Federal Tax Authority's Free Zone Persons guidance.
