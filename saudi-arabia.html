The Saudi market

A Structured Market for Long-Term Business

Saudi Arabia differs from the UAE and Bahrain in its approach to business entry. Establishing a presence requires consideration of business activity, ownership, regulatory requirements and operational readiness.

This is a market for businesses with a clear commercial direction and a structured approach to entering and operating in the Kingdom.

Is Saudi Arabia Right for Your Business?

When Saudi may fit

  • Long-term GCC expansion
  • Clear commercial and revenue model
  • Requirement for a Saudi market presence
  • Local customers, contracts or business opportunities
  • Readiness for ongoing regulatory requirements

When further assessment may be needed

  • Market is still being tested
  • Saudi operating model is not yet defined
  • Objective is mainly exploratory
  • Looking primarily for a quick, low-cost setup
  • Not yet ready for ongoing operational commitments

Frequently asked questions

Saudi Setup, Answered

Key questions to resolve before deciding on a structure or beginning an application.

Is MISA Investment Registration required for foreign investors?

Foreign investors are required to register with MISA before engaging in investment activities, subject to the applicable framework.

Can a foreign investor own 100% of a Saudi company?

This depends on the business activity and applicable requirements.

Is MISA Investment Registration the same as Commercial Registration?

No. Investment Registration and Commercial Registration are separate parts of the establishment process.

Do I need an office in Saudi Arabia?

Premises requirements depend on the business activity, structure and applicable requirements.

How much does it cost to establish a business in Saudi Arabia?

Costs vary according to the activity, structure, ownership, approvals, premises and operational requirements.

How long does Saudi company formation take?

The timeline varies according to the structure, documentation, Investment Registration, approvals and operational requirements.

Can Nikki Advisory assist after establishment?

Yes. Support can continue with relevant corporate, compliance and operational requirements.
