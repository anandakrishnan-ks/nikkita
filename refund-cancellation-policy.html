01

Service Cancellation

A client may request cancellation of a service by providing written notice to Nikki Advisory.

Cancellation terms may depend on the stage of the engagement, work already completed and any government or third-party costs incurred on the client's behalf.

02

Refund Eligibility

Refunds, where applicable, will be considered based on:

  • Whether the requested service has commenced.
  • The amount of work already completed.
  • Whether government, authority or third-party costs have already been incurred.
  • The specific payment and cancellation terms agreed in the quotation or engagement document.
03

Non-Refundable Amounts

The following may be non-refundable:

  • Government and authority fees already paid or processed.
  • Visa, licensing and other statutory charges.
  • Third-party service fees or expenses incurred on behalf of the client.
  • Services already completed or substantially performed.
  • Any other non-refundable amounts specifically identified in the agreed quotation or engagement terms.
04

Refund Processing

Where a refund is approved, it will generally be processed through the original payment method, subject to applicable payment-processing requirements.

The applicable refund amount and processing timeline will be communicated to the client at the time the refund is approved.

05

Changes in Government or Third-Party Requirements

Nikki Advisory is not responsible for refunds arising solely from changes, delays, rejection or additional requirements imposed by government authorities, licensing bodies, banks or other independent third parties.

Any refund will be assessed according to the work completed, costs incurred and terms agreed for the engagement.

06

How to Request a Cancellation or Refund

To request a cancellation or refund, please contact us in writing:

Email: info@nikkiadvisory.com

Please include your name, service details and relevant quotation or invoice reference.
