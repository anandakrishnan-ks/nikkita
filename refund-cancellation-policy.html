01

Cancellations & Eligibility

A client may request cancellation in writing. Eligibility and any refundable amount will be assessed under the agreed quotation or engagement terms, based on when the request is received and the work already completed. Costs that cannot be refunded are described below.

02

Non-Refundable Costs

Government and authority fees already paid or processed, visa and licensing charges, third-party expenses, completed or substantially performed services, and amounts identified as non-refundable in the agreed terms may not be refundable.

Changes, delays, rejection or additional requirements imposed by authorities, banks or other independent third parties do not by themselves create a right to a refund.

03

Approved Refunds

If a refund is approved, we will confirm the amount and expected processing time. It will generally be returned through the original payment method, subject to the payment provider's requirements.

04

Submit a Cancellation or Refund Request

Email info@nikkiadvisory.com with your name, service details and relevant quotation or invoice reference.
