Practical questions

PRO & Government Services FAQs

What PRO and government services are supported?

Support includes investor, employment and family visa assistance; Emirates ID, CPR and Iqama coordination; labour and immigration support; document clearing and attestation; licence renewals and amendments; and application coordination.

Are approvals and timelines guaranteed?

Applications remain subject to the relevant authority’s requirements and approvals. Documentation, eligibility and timelines vary by jurisdiction and individual circumstances.

How do I request PRO support?

Share the process, authority and jurisdiction involved. Nikki Advisory reviews the requirement, helps organise the relevant documentation, coordinates applications and follows up on the next steps.
