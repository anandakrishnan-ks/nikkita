Overview

What Is SPC Free Zone?

Sharjah Publishing City Free Zone (SPC Free Zone) is a Sharjah free zone established with a strong focus on publishing and creative industries, while also supporting a broad range of commercial, professional and digital businesses.

SPC offers a wide selection of approved business activities and digital setup solutions for entrepreneurs, startups, SMEs and international companies.

  • Publishing & E-Publishing
  • Printing & Distribution
  • Media & Creative Activities
  • Translation & Content Services
  • E-commerce
  • Trading & Distribution
  • Professional & Consulting Services
  • Technology & Digital Businesses
Publishing to digital business.SPC's publishing roots sit alongside a wider business environment.
Assess the Fit

Who May Consider SPC?

SPC may suit businesses looking for a flexible Sharjah free-zone setup with broad activity choices and digital incorporation.

Who May Consider SPC

  • Publishing and e-publishing businesses
  • Media and creative businesses
  • Content and translation companies
  • E-commerce and trading businesses
  • Consulting and professional services
  • Technology, digital businesses, startups and SMEs

When SPC May Not Be the Right Fit

  • Your business requires substantial industrial or manufacturing infrastructure.
  • You need large-scale specialised logistics facilities.
  • Your business requires a highly specialised financial jurisdiction.
  • Another UAE jurisdiction provides a stronger location or infrastructure fit.

The right jurisdiction should be selected according to the business activity, target market and operating requirements.

Licence & Activity Options

What Can You Set Up in SPC Free Zone?

SPC provides licensing options covering a broad range of approved business activities. The appropriate activity and licence combination should be confirmed before proceeding.

Publishing Licence

For approved publishing-related activities and content businesses.

E-Publishing Licence

For eligible digital publishing, e-books, online publications and electronic content.

Commercial Licence

For approved trading and commercial activities.

General Trading Licence

For eligible businesses involved in broader import, export and trading activities.

Service Licence

For approved professional, consultancy and service activities.

E-Commerce Licence

For approved online trading and e-commerce activities.

Company Structures

SPC Company Structure Options

Free Zone Company

A company can be established in SPC Free Zone under the applicable free-zone structure.

Individual Company

An eligible individual can establish a company as a single natural-person shareholder.

Branch / Corporate Structure

Existing companies may establish an appropriate branch or corporate structure, subject to requirements and documentation.

The appropriate structure depends on ownership, activity, existing corporate presence and business objectives.

Tax & Regulatory Considerations

Before Setting Up in SPC

Corporate Tax

SPC companies fall within the UAE Corporate Tax regime. Treatment depends on activities, income and Free Zone eligibility.

Qualifying Free Zone Person

The 0% Corporate Tax rate is not automatic; it applies to Qualifying Income of a Qualifying Free Zone Person, subject to conditions.

VAT

VAT registration and compliance should be assessed under applicable UAE VAT rules.

Mainland Business

An SPC Free Zone licence does not automatically provide unrestricted mainland operating rights.

Additional Approvals

Publishing, media, education, healthcare and other regulated activities may require additional approvals.

Workspace

SPC offers different workspace and facility options; requirements depend on the setup and activity.
