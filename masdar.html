Overview

Masdar Free Zone, located within Masdar City in Abu Dhabi, is a world-renowned hub for sustainability, renewable energy, and clean technology. It provides a progressive eco-friendly infrastructure combined with corporate tax benefits.

Masdar Highlights:

Sustainability Brand Power

  • Globally recognized sustainable city & clean tech brand
  • Proximity to Abu Dhabi International Airport & Abu Dhabi city
  • Access to R&D labs & green technology partners

Future-Proof Infrastructure

  • LEED-certified sustainable office buildings
  • Flexi-desk & custom commercial office solutions
  • Integrated clean-energy research ecosystem

100% Tax & Ownership

  • 100% foreign ownership & 0% personal tax
  • 100% profit & capital repatriation
  • Streamlined licensing & digital administration

Masdar City is the ideal location for ESG-driven corporate ventures and renewable tech.
