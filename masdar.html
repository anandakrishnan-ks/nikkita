Overview

What Is Masdar City Free Zone?

Masdar City Free Zone is an Abu Dhabi free zone built around innovation, sustainability, clean technology and research and development.

It is part of Masdar City's wider innovation ecosystem, supporting startups, SMEs, multinational companies, research organisations and technology-focused businesses.

Business Fit

Is Masdar City Right for Your Business?

Masdar City is particularly relevant when innovation, sustainability, technology or R&D forms part of the business model.

Suitable For

  • Clean-tech and renewable-energy businesses
  • Technology and AI businesses
  • Sustainability-focused companies
  • R&D businesses

Important Considerations

Before Setting Up in Masdar City

Corporate Tax

UAE Corporate Tax treatment depends on the entity, activities, income and free-zone conditions.

0% Corporate Tax

Free-zone status does not automatically provide a 0% rate; qualifying conditions apply.

VAT

Registration and compliance depend on applicable UAE VAT rules.

Mainland Business

A free-zone licence does not by itself grant unrestricted mainland operating rights.

Additional Approvals

Technology, research, energy, healthcare and other regulated activities may need approvals.

Specialised Programmes

Space and other sector-specific programmes may have separate eligibility requirements.

See Masdar City Free Zone licensing guidance and the Federal Tax Authority's free-zone guidance.
