Dubai Silicon Oasis (DSO) Company Formation

A technology-focused free zone and innovation park in Dubai designed for software, electronics, R&D, and digital enterprises.

Book a Consultation Talk to an Advisor

Overview

Dubai Silicon Oasis (DSO) is a government-backed integrated technology park combining business, residential, and academic infrastructure. It is designed to nurture technology-driven companies, research centers, and digital startups.

DSO Core Highlights:

Tech Innovation Ecosystem

  • Dedicated technology park & startup incubator (DTEC)
  • Proximity to Academic City & research institutes
  • High-tech infrastructure & data centers

Live-Work Community

  • Integrated commercial office towers & tech hubs
  • Residential communities, schools, & retail amenities
  • Flexible plug-and-play office desk packages

100% Tax & Ownership

  • 100% foreign ownership & capital repatriation
  • 0% personal tax & qualifying corporate tax exemptions
  • Fast digital registration & visa processing

Dubai Silicon Oasis provides a full tech ecosystem for digital platforms and innovation leaders.

Who Should Choose DSO?

DSO License Categories

Service License

IT & Consultancy
  • Hub: Tech Park / DTEC
  • Ownership: 100% Foreign
  • Office: Flexi / Dedicated
  • Best For: IT Consultants & SaaS

Commercial License

E-Commerce & Tech Goods
  • Hub: DSO Business Park
  • Ownership: 100% Foreign
  • Office: Flexi-Desk / Office
  • Best For: Tech Trading & Hardware

Industrial License

Assembly & Light Industry
  • Hub: DSO High Tech Zone
  • Ownership: 100% Foreign
  • Office: Light Industrial Unit
  • Best For: Hardware & Robotics

Typical setup cost range: AED 15,000 – AED 30,000+ depending on visa package and facility space.
