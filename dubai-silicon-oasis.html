Overview

What Is Dubai Silicon Oasis?

Dubai Silicon Oasis (DSO) is a technology-focused business district and free zone in Dubai, operating under the Dubai Integrated Economic Zones Authority (DIEZ).

DSO supports startups, SMEs and multinational companies, with facilities ranging from co-working spaces and dedicated offices to warehouses, industrial units and land for development.

Who May Consider DSO?

DSO may be relevant for businesses that value technology-oriented infrastructure, Dubai connectivity and room for operational growth.

  • Professional and business service companies
  • Technology-related trading businesses

When DSO May Not Be the Right Fit

  • Your primary requirement is the lowest-cost free-zone setup.
  • Your business does not benefit from DSO's technology and business ecosystem.
  • You require a highly specialised commodities or industry-specific jurisdiction.
  • Your business requires mainland operations without considering applicable licensing arrangements.

The right jurisdiction should be selected according to the business activity, target market and operational requirements.

Licence Types

DSO Licence Options

DSO provides three principal licence categories for approved business activities.

Service Licence

For approved professional and service activities.

Trade Licence

For approved activities related to the import, export and distribution of specified goods.

Industrial Licence

For approved activities involving importing raw materials, manufacturing, processing, assembling, packaging and exporting finished products.

The permitted activity should be confirmed before selecting the licence and company structure.

Company Structures

DSO Company Structure Options

Free Zone Company — FZCO

A limited-liability company incorporated in the DSO Free Zone with its own legal personality. Shareholders' liability is limited to their share capital.

Branch

A branch of a company incorporated outside the Free Zone. It has no separate legal personality from its parent, and its activities must fall within the parent's permitted activities.

Business Operation Permit / Business Permit

A special permit for businesses operating from qualifying properties within DSO's Administrative Zone using a Dubai Department of Economy and Tourism (DET) licence.

The appropriate structure depends on ownership, existing company, activity and the location from which the business will operate.

DSO Company Formation Requirements

Depending on the proposed structure and activity, requirements may include:

  • Passport copies of shareholders
  • Passport copy of manager/director, where applicable
  • Emirates ID and visa documents, where applicable
  • Proposed business activity
  • Company profile or business plan, where required
  • Corporate documents for corporate shareholders
  • UBO documentation
  • NOC for a UAE-resident manager, where applicable
  • Additional approvals for regulated activities

Requirements vary according to the activity, ownership structure and legal entity.
