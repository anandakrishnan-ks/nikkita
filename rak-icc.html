RAK ICC Offshore Company Formation

The UAE's premier international corporate centre for holding companies, property ownership, asset protection, and global investment.

Book a Consultation Talk to an Advisor

Overview

RAK ICC (Ras Al Khaimah International Corporate Centre) is a world-class offshore registry. UAE offshore entities are non-resident structures designed strictly for international business, asset holding, and wealth protection outside the UAE market.

RAK ICC Core Highlights:

Asset Protection & Holding

  • Hold shares in global subsidiaries & real estate
  • Dubai real estate holding via DLD MOU agreement
  • Confidential corporate register and UBO protection

Zero Office & Tax Benefits

  • No physical office space or lease required
  • 0% corporate tax & 0% personal tax exposure
  • 100% capital repatriation & multi-currency options

Key Limitations

  • Cannot trade directly within the onshore UAE market
  • Does NOT grant UAE residency visas
  • Requires a licensed Registered Agent for incorporation

RAK ICC is ideal for international asset isolation, holding structures, and wealth management.

Who Should Choose RAK ICC?

International holding companies owning shares in operating entities worldwide Holding Companies
Property investors holding real estate assets in Dubai (via DLD MOU) or internationally Real Estate Holding
Cross-border consulting, intellectual property (IP) holding, and asset isolation IP & Asset Isolation
