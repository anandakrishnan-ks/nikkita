Holding · Investment · Cross-Border
Overview

What Is RAK ICC?

RAK International Corporate Centre is an international corporate registry in Ras Al Khaimah for cross-border structuring, holding companies, investment vehicles, foundations and wealth-planning structures.

The appropriate entity and permitted use depend on the ownership, assets, purpose and applicable rules.

Registry Structure ≠ Operating Licence

For ordinary UAE operations, compare a suitable RAKEZ or mainland operating route.

Holding Companies Investment Structures Asset Ownership IP Holding Cross-Border Arrangements Joint Ventures Wealth Planning Foundations & Succession
Business Fit

Is RAK ICC Right for Your Business?

Start with what the entity needs to own, hold or achieve.

Suitable For

  • International entrepreneurs
  • Investors needing a holding structure
  • Parent or investment companies
  • Holding shares in other companies
  • Eligible asset ownership
  • Family wealth and succession planning
  • Cross-border corporate arrangements

May Not Be the Right Fit

  • Normal UAE trading or service operations
  • A UAE operating licence is required
  • Employment or residence visas through the entity are essential
  • Manufacturing, warehousing or physical facilities are required
  • A mainland or conventional Free Zone company fits better

The RAK ICC Decision

Choose a Structure for a Clear Purpose

RAK ICC is most relevant for holding, investment, asset ownership, wealth planning or cross-border structuring—not day-to-day UAE operations.

Is an international or holding structure needed?
Will it own shares, investments or eligible assets?
Are multiple countries or entities involved?
Will it support long-term ownership or succession?
RAK ICC should be selected for a corporate purpose—not as a substitute for a UAE operating company.
Important Considerations

Before Establishing a RAK ICC Structure

UAE Operations

A standard international company is not a conventional UAE operating licence.

Residence Visas

Do not assume an international company provides ordinary operating-company visa eligibility.

Banking

Bank-account approval is not guaranteed; banks conduct separate due diligence.

Corporate Tax

Tax treatment depends on activities, income and applicable UAE rules—not the registry name alone.

International Tax

Other countries' rules may apply to shareholders, assets, income or related businesses.

UBO & AML

Maintain applicable beneficial-owner and anti-money-laundering compliance.

Registered Agent

Incorporation and administration follow the applicable Registered Agent framework.

See RAK ICC's regulations and the Federal Tax Authority's Corporate Tax FAQs. Specific legal and tax treatment needs individual assessment.
