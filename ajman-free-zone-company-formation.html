Overview

What Is Ajman Free Zone?

Ajman Free Zone (AFZ) is an established UAE free zone offering business setup solutions across commercial, service, e-commerce and industrial activities.

Its location provides access to Ajman Port and Dubai and Sharjah airports, making it relevant for businesses involved in trading, distribution, services and regional operations.

Key Business Areas

Trading & Distribution E-commerce Professional & Consultancy Manufacturing & Industrial Food & Beverage Health & Beauty Construction Education Technology & Approved Activities
Business Fit

Who May Consider Ajman Free Zone?

Ajman Free Zone may suit businesses seeking flexible setup options, operational facilities and a cost-conscious UAE business environment.

Best Suited For

  • Trading and distribution businesses
  • Import, export and general trading companies
  • E-commerce businesses
  • Consulting and professional services
  • Manufacturing and industrial businesses
  • Businesses requiring warehouses or facilities
  • Startups and SMEs
  • Access to Ajman, Sharjah and Dubai markets

When It May Not Fit

  • A Dubai-based address or ecosystem is essential
  • A highly specialised financial or industry jurisdiction is required
  • The primary need is a particular mainland operating structure
  • Another UAE jurisdiction offers more suitable infrastructure

Important Considerations

Before Setting Up in Ajman Free Zone

Corporate Tax

Tax treatment depends on activities, income and eligibility under Free Zone rules.

0% Corporate Tax

The rate is not automatic and applies only to qualifying income when conditions are met.

VAT

Registration and compliance should be assessed under the applicable UAE VAT rules.

Mainland Business

A free-zone licence does not automatically provide unrestricted mainland operating rights.

Additional Approvals

Regulated or specialised activities may require authority approvals or NOCs.

Facility Requirements

Office, warehouse or industrial needs depend on the activity and business model.
