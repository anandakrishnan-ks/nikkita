Overview

What Is Meydan Free Zone?

Meydan Free Zone is a Dubai-based free zone designed to simplify company formation through a digitally enabled setup platform, while providing a broad range of commercial and professional activities.

The free zone is positioned for entrepreneurs, startups, SMEs and established businesses seeking a flexible Dubai free-zone structure.

Important Considerations

Before Setting Up in Meydan Free Zone

Corporate Tax

Meydan Free Zone companies fall within the UAE Corporate Tax regime. Applicable treatment depends on activities, income and eligibility under the Free Zone rules.

0% Corporate Tax

The 0% rate is not automatic. It applies to Qualifying Income of a Qualifying Free Zone Person, subject to the applicable conditions.

VAT

VAT registration and compliance should be assessed according to the applicable UAE VAT rules.

Mainland Business

A Meydan Free Zone licence does not automatically provide unrestricted mainland operating rights. The appropriate arrangement should be assessed according to the intended activity.

Additional Approvals

Certain activities may require third-party or regulatory approvals.

Workspace & Visas

Requirements depend on the selected licence and business requirements.

For current tax rules, see the Federal Tax Authority's Free Zone Persons guidance.
