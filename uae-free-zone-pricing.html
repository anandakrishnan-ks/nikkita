02 / Process

The sequence from decision to setup.

Allow approximately 2 – 7 working days, subject to documentation, authority review and any required approvals.

The authority, licence activity and visa allocation are the key decisions to confirm before comparing packages.

03 / Documents

What to prepare first.

The initial documents below help start the application. Authorities may request additional information for your activity or structure.

  • Passport copy
  • Photograph
  • Application form
04 / Tax & considerations

Keep the ongoing obligations in view.

Tax considerations

UAE Corporate Tax is generally 9% on taxable income above AED 375,000. Qualifying Free Zone Persons may have different treatment for qualifying income. VAT is 5%; mandatory registration generally applies when taxable supplies and imports exceed AED 375,000.

General guidance only; confirm current rules and eligibility with the authority or a qualified adviser. UAE Federal Tax Authority
