Company Liquidation

Company liquidation and business closure support including license cancellation and regulatory coordination across UAE, Saudi Arabia, and Bahrain.

Book a Consultation Talk to an Advisor

Overview

Closing a business requires proper coordination to ensure regulatory obligations and procedural requirements are completed correctly across all relevant authorities.

Our Company Liquidation execution pillars:

Liquidator & Filings

  • Board resolution drafting for company dissolution
  • Appointment of registered auditor / liquidator
  • Liquidator report generation & submission

Clearances & Tax

  • FTA Tax Deregistration & NOC obtaining
  • MOHRE / Labour & Immigration clearances
  • Customs & utility provider NOC certificates

License Cancellation

  • Public notice newspaper publication (where required)
  • Final Commercial Register de-registration certificate
  • Visa cancellations & bank account closure coordination

Structured liquidation protects directors and shareholders from ongoing statutory liabilities.

Service Deliverables

Initial Stage

Phase 1: Dissolution Notice
  • Resolutions: Shareholder Dissolution
  • Liquidator: Approved Auditor Appointed
  • Publication: Public Notice Period
  • Initial Approval: DED / Freezone Submission

Clearance Phase

Phase 2: NOC Gathering
  • Tax NOC: FTA Deregistration Certificate
  • Labour & Imm: Quota Cancellation NOC
  • Customs: Code Deactivation Letter
  • Utilities: DEWA / Lease Settlement

Final Striking Off

Phase 3: Formal Strike Off
  • Audit Report: Final Liquidation Report
  • Certificate: Official De-registration
  • Bank Account: Account Closure Letter
  • Visas: All Visas Canceled

Liquidation timelines vary based on jurisdiction (Mainland vs. Free Zone) and notice periods.

Why Structured Liquidation Matters

Failing to properly liquidate an entity can leave shareholders liable for automatic license renewal fees, visa penalties, and tax fines.

  • Obtain formal de-registration certificates
  • Clear tax liabilities with Federal Tax Authority
  • Prevent future blacklisting of shareholders or directors
