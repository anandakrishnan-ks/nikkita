Overview

What Is JAFZA?

Jebel Ali Free Zone (JAFZA) is an established Dubai free zone and major business and logistics hub connected to Jebel Ali Port and Dubai's wider trade infrastructure.

JAFZA is particularly relevant to businesses involved in the movement, storage, manufacturing and distribution of goods, while also supporting services, e-commerce and international business activities.

Business Fit

Is JAFZA Right for Your Business?

JAFZA can be particularly suitable for businesses that require trade connectivity, logistics infrastructure or physical operational facilities.

Best Suited For

  • Import & Export BusinessesCompanies moving goods through international trade routes.
  • Trading & DistributionBusinesses requiring commercial and distribution infrastructure.
  • Logistics & FreightEligible logistics and third-party logistics businesses.
  • Warehousing & FulfilmentBusinesses requiring physical storage or distribution facilities.
  • Manufacturing & IndustrialCompanies requiring appropriate industrial infrastructure.
  • E-Commerce & Supply ChainsBusinesses requiring fulfilment, distribution and regional connectivity.

When Another Jurisdiction May Be Better

  • The primary objective is the lowest-cost company formation
  • Only a simple service or consultancy setup with minimal physical requirements is needed
  • JAFZA's trade, logistics or industrial infrastructure is not relevant
  • A specialised financial, technology or professional-services jurisdiction is more appropriate
Select around the operating model

The right jurisdiction should be selected according to business activity, facility requirements and the operating model.

Company Structures

Company Structure Options

FZE One shareholder

A single-shareholder limited-liability company. The shareholder may be an individual or a company.

FZCO Multiple shareholders

A multiple-shareholder limited-liability company, with individuals, companies or both.

Branch

An existing UAE or foreign company may establish a JAFZA branch, subject to requirements.

Offshore Company

An offshore structure is available for eligible purposes through a JAFZA-registered agent. Explore JAFZA Offshore.

Important Considerations

Before Setting Up in JAFZA

Facility Requirement

JAFZA states that company formation requires a leased facility. The facility must suit the activity and operations.

Corporate Tax

JAFZA companies fall within the UAE Corporate Tax regime. Treatment depends on activities, income and eligibility under the Free Zone rules.

0% Corporate Tax

The 0% rate is not automatic. It applies to Qualifying Income of a Qualifying Free Zone Person, subject to conditions.

VAT

VAT registration and compliance should be assessed under applicable UAE VAT rules.

Mainland Business

A JAFZA Free Zone licence does not automatically provide unrestricted mainland operating rights.

Additional Approvals

Industrial, logistics, environmental and other regulated activities may require further approvals.

Operational Requirements

Warehouse, industrial and logistics activities may involve facility, environmental, safety and operational requirements.

See JAFZA's facility and formation requirements and the Federal Tax Authority's Free Zone Persons guidance.
