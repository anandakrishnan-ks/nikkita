Saudi Arabia Business Setup Cost & Process

Saudi market entry is a structured, compliance-driven undertaking suited to long-term operational plans. See the stages and likely requirements before seeking a tailored quotation.

Request a Detailed Quotation
01 / Cost

Understand the indicative investment.

The range is a planning guide, not a fixed package or final quotation.

Indicative setupSAR 25,000 Onwards

Varies by approvals, activity and structure. Approvals or documentation may extend the timeline.

T&C Apply

02 / Process

The sequence from decision to setup.

Allow approximately 4 – 8 weeks, subject to documentation, authority review and any required approvals.

Office setup is required. This is not a quick or low-cost market entry; plan for approvals and operational readiness.

  1. 01Business structure definition
  2. 02Investment licence approval via the Ministry of Investment
  3. 03Trade name & documentation
  4. 04Commercial Registration (CR)
  5. 05Government registrations
  6. 06Office setup & bank account
  7. 07Operational activation
03 / Documents

What to prepare first.

The initial documents below help start the application. Authorities may request additional information for your activity or structure.

  • Passport copy
  • Business plan
  • Company profile / parent documents
04 / Tax & considerations

Keep the ongoing obligations in view.

Tax considerations

Corporate income tax is generally 20% on the non-Saudi ownership share of taxable income. Zakat is generally 2.5% of the applicable base for Saudi/GCC ownership. Standard VAT is 15%; registration depends on applicable thresholds.
