ADGM Company Formation in Abu Dhabi

A globally recognized financial centre on Al Maryah Island operating under English Common Law, ideal for funds, family offices, and holding structures.

Book a Consultation Talk to an Advisor

Overview

ADGM (Abu Dhabi Global Market) is an international financial center operating directly under English Common Law with independent ADGM Courts, making it an elite choice for financial institutions, private equity, asset managers, and family holding companies.

Three Pillars of ADGM:

English Common Law Legal System

  • Direct application of English Common Law & precedent
  • Independent ADGM Courts staffed by international judges
  • High legal security for institutional equity investors

Dual Regulatory Authorities

  • Registration Authority (RA) for corporate & non-financial entities
  • Financial Services Regulatory Authority (FSRA) for banks & funds
  • Global transparency & anti-money-laundering alignment

Al Maryah Island Prime Location

  • Based on Al Maryah Island & Reem Island expansion zone
  • Flexibility for Special Purpose Vehicles (SPVs) & Holding entities
  • 100% foreign ownership & 0% personal tax

ADGM offers maximum structural protection for global funds, asset managers, and family wealth.
