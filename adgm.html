Overview

What Is ADGM?

Abu Dhabi Global Market (ADGM) is an international financial centre in Abu Dhabi with its own legal and regulatory framework based on English common law.

ADGM accommodates financial, non-financial and retail businesses. Its structures and activities span financial services, wealth management, professional services, technology, holding companies, foundations and other approved activities.

Financial services follow a separate path

Regulated financial applicants engage with the FSRA before the Registration Authority (RA) incorporation stage. Review the official FSRA process.

Business Fit

Is ADGM Right for Your Business?

ADGM is particularly relevant where the legal framework, regulatory environment or corporate structure is an important part of the decision.

Suitable For

  • Financial services businesses
  • Asset and wealth management
  • Investment and holding structures
  • FinTech and technology businesses
  • Family offices and private wealth
  • Professional and corporate services

May Not Be the Right Fit If

  • The lowest-cost formation is the main priority
  • A specialised financial-centre environment is unnecessary
  • A general commercial free zone better suits the activity
  • The business needs substantial manufacturing, warehousing or industrial infrastructure

Licence & Activity Options

Licence and Activity Options in ADGM

ADGM broadly categorises businesses as financial, non-financial or retail. Technology startups may have a specific eligible route within its ecosystem.

Financial Services

For regulated banking, asset management, capital markets, money services and other financial activities. The FSRA process starts before RA incorporation; final permission follows the applicable conditions.

Non-Financial / Professional

For approved consulting, corporate solutions, head offices, holding-related and other professional activities.

Retail

For eligible retail and consumer-facing activities permitted within ADGM.

Technology & Startups

The Tech Startup Licence has specific eligibility requirements, including a Hub71 eligibility letter before application.

Select the activity carefully

Different activities follow different approvals and licensing requirements. See ADGM setup categories and Tech Startup Licence guidance.
